🍗Delicious Chicken Biryani Recipe🍗

Chicken Biryani is a traditional and popular dish. It is loved by everyone.

Cooking is an art, and Biryani is love.

Ingredients

1. 1 kg Chicken
2. 2 cups Rice
3. Spices
4. Yogurt

Cooking Steps

1. Marinate the chicken.
2. Boil the rice.
3. Cook chicken with spices.
4. Layer rice and chicken and cook on low flame.
